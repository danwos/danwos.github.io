I'm Daniel Woste, a software developer, and what I like building most are tools that make other people's work easier. The problems that interest me most are those of large, distributed teams: how do hundreds of people keep track of requirements, tests, decisions and the knowledge in between?

That's why I founded useblocks, where I helped build Sphinx-Needs and many other docs-as-code tools as team lead. They run in large engineering projects today, especially in the automotive industry. These days I support useblocks as an advisor.

With danwos I now build my own tools – small, focused, each for exactly one problem – and advise teams that want to get their documentation, their quality and their decisions under control.

I live with my family and our three kids in North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, near Dortmund. Outside of work, I volunteer – today on the organizing team of a children's market, earlier in refugee support.
