problem: |
  Customer wishes come in many ways: from projects, conversations, support requests. It's easy to lose track of where a wish came from, which problem is behind it and what finally came of it.

  Product planning at useblocks needed one place where wishes, problems and features are connected – traceable from the first customer conversation into development.
approach: |
  I built the demand planning with Sphinx-Needs. Customer wishes, problems and features are objects of their own, linked to each other. A workflow defines how a wish is reviewed, assigned to a problem and turned into a feature. A CI system builds and checks everything on every change.

  Features are described from the user's point of view: Who needs what, and why? From there, they are broken down and derived into tasks for the development teams. The feature roadmaps come from the same data.
result: |
  Every customer wish is traceable: which problem it belongs to, which feature solves it and when it ships. The development teams see why they build something, and the roadmap stays connected to the wishes behind it.
